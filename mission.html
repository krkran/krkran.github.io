02 · Missionउद्देश्य

A voice for the underrepresented.

And a resource that helps all of Australia learn from its many cultures.

The mission, in one breath

KRNBRY Studio exists to give voice to underrepresented and marginalised communities across Australia through authentic, community-centred storytelling in all its forms.

That includes culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) communities, rural and remote Australians, refugee and asylum seeker communities, and LGBTQIA+ people. We create original work and platform the work of others. Everything we produce is made to be accessible to community, and any surplus goes back into more work, more events and more voices. None of it is distributed to members.

Our goal

Content for education and the growth of multicultural Australia.

Storytelling is how people learn about each other. Our long-term goal is to build a library of content that teaches, celebrates and connects, so that multicultural communities feel seen and the wider community understands them better.

Three pillars

How we'll pursue the mission.

These are the aims the organisation was set up to pursue. A few are underway. Most are still ahead of us, and we'll grow into them step by step.

  1. Produce and support documentary, podcast, short-form video and digital content that amplifies the stories of underrepresented and marginalised communities.
  2. Tell the stories of health professionals, health students and healthcare communities, with a focus on rural and remote workers, CALD health professionals, and communities facing barriers to care.
  3. Create content that improves health literacy and understanding of the healthcare system in multicultural communities.
  4. Build a library of educational content that helps Australians learn about the cultures around them.
  5. Platform filmmakers, photographers and digital storytellers from underrepresented communities.
  1. Support and resource emerging artists from underrepresented communities across film, fashion, music, theatre, performance and photography.
  2. Present and promote creative work by artists from marginalised backgrounds.
  3. Develop workshops, school incursions and learning resources on storytelling, media literacy and cultural identity.
  4. Involve young people from multicultural backgrounds as co-creators, not just audiences.
  5. Help medical students and early-career health professionals from underrepresented backgrounds build media and health communication skills.
  1. Hold community events such as fashion shows, music nights, performance evenings and cultural showcases that celebrate underrepresented artists.
  2. Where events are ticketed, direct ticket revenue to the artists and performers involved.
  3. Create welcoming spaces, both pop-up and online, for storytelling, cultural exchange and connection.
  4. Document events on camera, building a lasting record of multicultural life in Australia.

The story so far

Started by a med student who makes community media.

KRNBRY Studio sits at the meeting point of medicine, community media and cultural storytelling. The idea is simple: the stories of underrepresented communities deserve to be told with craft, care and real independence.

We want those stories to do more than entertain. We want them to teach. To help a newly arrived family understand the health system, help a classroom understand a culture they haven't met, and help young creators see that their story is worth telling.

Future plans

Ideas we're working towards.

None of these are running yet. They're plans in development. If one speaks to you, as a collaborator, artist, school or funder, press I'm interested and tell us.

Education

Multilingual explainers

Short videos explaining Australian systems, like healthcare, schooling and support services, in community languages and made with community members.

Education

Culture in Focus

A series introducing the traditions, food, festivals and everyday life of Australia's many cultures, told by the people who live them. Designed to be used in classrooms.

Education

Schools storytelling program

Workshops and incursions exploring media literacy, cultural identity and storytelling, for metropolitan, regional and rural schools.

Education

Health communication workshops

Media and storytelling skills for medical students and early-career health professionals from underrepresented backgrounds.

Photo documentary

Diaspora Portraits

A photo series capturing the faces, stories and lives of diaspora communities across Australia.

Podcast

Community Voices

An interview series where multicultural community members talk about identity, belonging, health and life in Australia. Bilingual in Nepali and English.

Podcast

Medicine from the Outside

Long-form conversations with CALD health professionals about their journeys through Australian medicine: the dual identity, the barriers and the breakthroughs.

Live event

CALD Fashion Showcase

A community fashion show for CALD designers, with ongoing support from production help to digital lookbooks. Ticket revenue would go to the artists.

Live event

Late Night Sessions

DJ sets and live music nights celebrating underrepresented artists, streamed online so more people can tune in.

Theatre

Community theatre program

Helping emerging CALD theatre makers with production resources, venue access and community audiences.

Creators

Access to equipment, mentorship and distribution for CALD filmmakers, photographers and digital creators. No experience needed.

Community

Community spaces and partnerships

Low-barrier gatherings, in person and online, built with cultural associations, health services, diaspora groups and advocacy bodies. If your work serves underrepresented communities, get in touch.
