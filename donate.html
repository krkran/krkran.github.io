Too many stories are told about communities, not with them.

We want to change that. KRNBRY Studio exists to give voice to underrepresented and marginalised communities across Australia, and to turn their stories into content people can actually learn from.

That means documentaries that explain, podcasts that open up conversations, and creative work that builds pride in cultural identity. Anything we earn goes back into making more of it.

Our mission

A voice for the underrepresented.

And a resource that helps all of Australia learn from its many cultures.

“

KRNBRY Studio exists to give voice to underrepresented and marginalised communities across Australia through authentic, community-centred storytelling in all its forms.

That includes culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) communities, rural and remote Australians, refugee and asylum seeker communities, and LGBTQIA+ people. We create original work and platform the work of others. Everything we produce is made to be accessible to community, and any surplus goes back into more work, more events and more voices. None of it is distributed to members.

Our goal

Content for education and the growth of multicultural Australia.

Storytelling is how people learn about each other. Our long-term goal is to build a library of content that teaches, celebrates and connects, so that multicultural communities feel seen and the wider community understands them better.

Educate

Explainers and resources that improve media literacy and health literacy, and help people navigate life in Australia.

Celebrate

Treat cultural identity as a strength, and give artists from underrepresented backgrounds a real platform.

Connect

Build understanding between cultures, generations and places, from city suburbs to rural towns.

Grow

Help young people and emerging creators build the skills to tell their own communities' stories.

Three pillars

How we'll pursue the mission.

●These are the aims the organisation was set up to pursue. A few are underway. Most are still ahead of us, and we'll grow into them step by step.
01

Media and storytelling

  • Produce and support documentary, podcast, short-form video and digital content that amplifies the stories of underrepresented and marginalised communities.
  • Tell the stories of health professionals, health students and healthcare communities, with a focus on rural and remote workers, CALD health professionals, and communities facing barriers to care.
  • Create content that improves health literacy and understanding of the healthcare system in multicultural communities.
  • Build a library of educational content that helps Australians learn about the cultures around them.
  • Platform filmmakers, photographers and digital storytellers from underrepresented communities.
02

Arts, creative practice and education

  • Support and resource emerging artists from underrepresented communities across film, fashion, music, theatre, performance and photography.
  • Present and promote creative work by artists from marginalised backgrounds.
  • Develop workshops, school incursions and learning resources on storytelling, media literacy and cultural identity.
  • Involve young people from multicultural backgrounds as co-creators, not just audiences.
  • Help medical students and early-career health professionals from underrepresented backgrounds build media and health communication skills.
03

Live events and community activation

  • Hold community events such as fashion shows, music nights, performance evenings and cultural showcases that celebrate underrepresented artists.
  • Where events are ticketed, direct ticket revenue to the artists and performers involved.
  • Create welcoming spaces, both pop-up and online, for storytelling, cultural exchange and connection.
  • Document events on camera, building a lasting record of multicultural life in Australia.
About us

Medicine, media and culture, together.

An Australian not-for-profit built from lived experience, and a belief that every community's story deserves to be told with care.

Where it began

CityVision TV

Before KRNBRY Studio, our work started with CityVision TV, an Australian online platform serving the Nepali diaspora through podcasts, documentary-style interviews and community storytelling. Making media from inside a community shapes everything we do now.

KRNBRY Studio is an Australian not-for-profit founded at the meeting point of medicine, community media and cultural storytelling.

It was started by someone training in medicine who is also a community media maker, with deep roots in the Nepali diaspora in Australia. The idea is simple: the stories of underrepresented communities deserve to be told with craft, care and real independence.

We want those stories to do more than entertain. We want them to teach. To help a newly arrived family understand the health system, help a classroom understand a culture they haven't met, and help young creators see that their story is worth telling.

We're not-for-profit by design. Every dollar goes back into community content, programs, events and supporting artists. No surplus leaves the organisation.

What we stand for

Our values.

Authenticity

We tell stories from the inside, centring the people who live the experience, not those watching from a distance.

Equity

We seek out the voices least heard in mainstream media: rural communities, CALD youth, migrant health professionals.

Cultural pride

Cultural identity is a strength, not a footnote. Diversity isn't a challenge to manage. It's the story worth telling.

Community

We exist to serve the communities we come from: multicultural, medical, rural and everything in between.

Independence

Our creative decisions aren't shaped by funders, institutions or professional hierarchies.

Craft

High production quality is a form of respect for the people whose stories we carry.
